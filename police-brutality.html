IPOA Confirms 65 Deaths, Later Widens Count to 127

Protest ViolenceIPOA

The Independent Policing Oversight Authority's July 2025 monitoring report tied 65 confirmed deaths and 342 civilian injuries to police handling of protests between June and July 2025, attending 61 autopsies to verify causes of death. IPOA later disclosed that combined with the 2024 Finance Bill protests and June 2025 memorial demonstrations, the total death toll across all three waves reached 127. The authority found officers used disproportionate force, deployed without visible name tags or service numbers, and in some cases concealed vehicle plates and rank insignia.

Source: The Star (Jul 2025) · Kenyans.co.ke · IPOA Official Report

The Death of Albert Ojwang in Police Custody

Custodial Death

Blogger and teacher Albert Ojwang was arrested in June 2025 over a social media post criticising a senior police official, then died in custody two days later. Police initially stated he had hit his head against a cell wall. An independent autopsy found injuries consistent with assault. His death became the catalyst for weeks of nationwide demonstrations.

Source: Wikipedia — 2025 Kenyan protests · Daily Nation

Boniface Kariuki — A Mask Vendor Shot on Camera

Documented Incident

Boniface Kariuki, a face-mask vendor with no apparent involvement in the protest, was shot in the head at close range by an officer in Nairobi on June 17, 2025. The shooting was captured on video and circulated widely. He was declared brain-dead and died of his injuries on June 30. Two officers were arrested that day; IPOA's investigation led to further charges against the officer directly responsible.

Source: Wikipedia — 2025 Kenyan protests

Bridgit Njoki, 12 — Killed Watching Television on Saba Saba Day

Civilian Casualty

On July 7, 2025 — Saba Saba Day, the 35th anniversary of Kenya's 1990 pro-democracy march — police used gunfire and tear gas against demonstrators. A stray bullet pierced the roof of a nearby house and struck 12-year-old Bridgit Njoki as she watched television. She died shortly after. 41 people were killed and 29 injured on that single day alone.

Source: Wikipedia — 2025 Kenyan protests

What IPOA Is Recommending — and What Hasn't Been Implemented

Reform Agenda

IPOA's report called for: body-worn cameras on crowd-control officers; mandatory visible name tags and service numbers; medical responders deployed alongside enforcement units; functional County Policing Authorities; and a single national Public Order Management Policy. As of the report's release, none of these had been implemented. Accountability to the Core will track these commitments going forward.

Source: People Daily · IPOA
