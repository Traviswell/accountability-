Our Manifesto

Built for Kenya.
By Kenyans.

A decentralized civil oversight architecture that translates local community asset failures into transparent verification ledgers — ensuring developmental parity across regional boundaries.

🎯 Our Mission

To translate local community infrastructure failures into transparent, verifiable public ledgers — ensuring that no county, ward, or citizen is left without recourse when the government fails to deliver.

⚙️ What We Track

Infrastructure collapse, police accountability, public debt mismanagement, corruption cases, and policy gaps that directly affect citizen rights — all sourced from official oversight bodies, not social media rumour.

🌐 Platform Philosophy

We believe transparency is infrastructure. Every citizen deserves access to verifiable data about how their taxes are spent, why their roads are broken, and who is accountable when systems fail.

📍 Where We Focus

Starting in Ngong Ward, Kajiado County — ground zero for the Matasia drainage crisis — and expanding outward to track national-level failures including the AI Bill 2026 and debt trajectory.
